Give Me a Break Privacy Policy

Effective date: [Date]

Give Me a Break does not collect, share or sell any information about you or your child. Everything about your child stays on your device. The app goes online only to check your subscription with the app store.

Give Me a Break ("the app") is made by RE Inv HoldCo LLC. This policy explains what the app does with information.

What the app keeps on the device

To work, the app keeps a few things in its own private storage on the device:

  • Your grown-up PIN, stored in scrambled form, never in plain text
  • Your child's grade and the screen-time settings you choose
  • The list of apps you choose to limit
  • Any words or questions you add
  • Which quiz questions were answered correctly, so missed ones come back for practice

This information never leaves the device. Deleting the app deletes it.

What the app does not do

The app has no accounts and no sign-in. It contains no advertising, no analytics and no third-party tracking tools. It does not collect names, email addresses, locations, photos, voice recordings, contacts or device identifiers. Nothing about your child's use of the device, such as which apps they open, how long they watch, or how they do on quizzes, is ever sent anywhere.

Subscriptions and payments

Give Me a Break offers a free trial followed by a paid subscription. Subscriptions are sold and billed by the app store you downloaded the app from (Google Play, or the Amazon Appstore on Fire tablets). The store collects your payment details and handles billing, renewals and cancellations under its own privacy policy. We never see or receive your card number, name, address or email from the store.

To know whether to unlock the full app, Give Me a Break connects to the store to check whether your subscription or trial is active. This check is the only reason the app uses the internet. It sends no information about your child.

You can cancel anytime in your Google Play or Amazon account. Before your free trial ends, the app shows a reminder on the device.

Permissions the app uses, and why

  • Usage access: lets the app see which app is on screen, so it can count time spent in the apps you chose to limit. This is used only on the device and is not recorded or sent anywhere.
  • Display over other apps: lets the app show the break screen and quiz on top of a limited app when time is up.
  • Notifications (optional): lets the app tell you if protection has been turned off, and remind you before a free trial ends.
  • Internet: used only to check your subscription with the app store, as described above.

Sight words are played from recordings built into the app. Other questions may be read aloud using the device's built-in text-to-speech feature. The app does not record audio.

Feedback emails

If you tap "Send feedback," the app opens your own email app with a draft that includes the app version and device model. Nothing is sent unless you send it. We use feedback emails only to answer you and improve the app.

Children's privacy

The app is designed for families with children. It does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13, so it does not collect personal information from children as defined by the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). A parent sets up the app, controls it with a PIN, and is the one who subscribes.

Question content

Some quiz questions come from the National Assessment of Educational Progress (NAEP), U.S. Department of Education, and from the U.S. Citizenship and Immigration Services (USCIS) civics test. These are public-domain materials. Neither agency endorses this app.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new effective date. If the app ever starts collecting information, we will ask for a parent's permission first.
